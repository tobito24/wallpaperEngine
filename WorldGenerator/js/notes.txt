
Beschreibung:

    This background image creates a random Pokemon-like landscape every time.

    There are several options to customize the image for you.
    All options are accessible either via Wallparer Engine or the built-in GUI.
    You can:
        - increase/decrease the chance of certain tiles.
        - decrease the "Accuracy" to get more performance.
        Be careful, errors can occur in the image (marked with the color red).
        - align the entire image however you like.
        - change the total number of tiles.
        - use "Autostart" to decide whether the next landscape should be started immediately after a completed landscape.

    I would like to place a special focus on the highlight mode.
    Mark an area on the screen with the mouse to have an influence on the next landscape.
    FREEZE_MODE: Highlighted area is transferred to the next screen.
    WRITE_MODE: Highlighted area is standardized to the next screen.

    I hope you like the wallpaper.
    Tobito
