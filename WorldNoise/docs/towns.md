# Towns: placement, terrain, buildings

Part of [WorldNoise](../CLAUDE.md). Source folder: `src/town/`. A town reads the base ground ([terrain.md](terrain.md)); the
road layer reads the town nodes ([roads.md](roads.md)), never the other way round. Design reasons: [idea.md](../idea.md),
Paths and towns.

## Constants

`config/constants.ts`: `TOWN_CELL_PROBABILITY`, `TOWN_DESIGNED_SHARE`, `TOWN_MIN_LAND_SHARE`, `TOWN_RING_WIDTH_TILES`,
`TOWN_SAMPLE_STRIDE_TILES`, `TOWN_CACHE_MAX_ENTRIES`,
`TOWN_MARGIN_TILES`, `TOWN_GATE_STRAIGHT_EDGE_BEFORE_TILES`, `TOWN_GATE_STRAIGHT_EDGE_AFTER_TILES`, `TOWN_OUTLINE_WOBBLE_TILES`, `TOWN_BUILDING_MARGIN_TILES`, `TOWN_BUILDING_STREET_MARGIN_TILES`, `TOWN_DOOR_PATH_MAX_TILES`, `TOWN_HOUSES_PER_CHUNK`.
`CELL_SIZE_CHUNKS` and `CELL_BUFFER_CHUNKS` are described in [roads.md](roads.md).

## Modules

- **`town/townShapes.ts`** (new) — the footprint shapes as chunk offsets inside the inner 3 x 3 chunks of a cell: `large`
  (3 x 3), `tall`, `wide` (2 x 3, 3 x 2), `L` and `T` (5 chunks, four rotations each), `O` (the 3 x 3 without its centre chunk, 8 chunks; the centre is a **hole**, `TownShape.holes`) and the small
  2 x 2 at its four positions. Each shape has a `kind`; `TOWN_SHAPE_KIND_WEIGHTS` gives the weight of every kind (small 30, large 15, tall 15, wide 15, L 15, T 10, O 8).
- **`town/townPlacement.ts`** (new) — `townOfCell(cx, cy)`: a pure function of the cell and the base ground, cached. A cell
  wants a town with probability `TOWN_CELL_PROBABILITY` (0.7); the kinds are put into a weighted random order by hash (key `-ln(1 - u) / weight`, smallest first), then the rotations or positions of a kind are tried in a hash order; a shape is taken when at
  least `TOWN_MIN_LAND_SHARE` (70 %, was 97 % until 2026-11-18) of the sampled tiles of its core chunks (every `TOWN_SAMPLE_STRIDE_TILES`, 2, tile) are land of the base
  ground; if none of a kind fits, the next kind in the order is tried, otherwise the cell keeps a plain waypoint. The plateau level is
  the most common land level of the base ground in the footprint, at most the level whose water
  clearance (`waterClearanceTiles`, 3 tiles for level 3, 5 for level 4) is free of water around the core chunks and their margin (`TOWN_MARGIN_TILES` more, because water in the margin becomes land and counts like water at the edge), so a coastal town
  is level 2 (a level 3 plateau needs two tiles of level 2 between it and water for the two cliff rings; before, a coastal town of
  level 3 touched water directly: 154 tiles in 64 x 64 chunks, now 0, and the natural terrain never has it), the node of the town is the centre of the chunk of the
  footprint nearest to its centroid (so it lies inside an L or T), and a hash makes `TOWN_DESIGNED_SHARE` (20 %) of the
  towns designed. Measured on 14 400 cells with 5 x 5 chunk cells (before the 7 x 7 cell): towns in 41 % (large 542, medium 3 286, small 2 084), designed 20.4 %,
  plateau levels 1 / 2 / 3 in 799 / 1 336 / 3 777 towns with three levels (towns stand inland, where the land is high; with four levels
  and the clearance on 4 096 chunks: 7 / 46 / 10 / 6 of 69 towns), 0.56 ms per cell.
- The **hole** of an `O` town (zone `hole`, 2026-11-18): the centre chunk is plateau at the plateau level (`inside`, water becomes land) with the ground of its biome (no paving, no wobble), is not part of the core (no lots, layout or town biome) and the decoration may grow there like on normal land (`decoPlacement` leaves `hole` out of its town mask); the through streets cross it. Measured on 15 O towns (40 x 40 cells): 3 840 hole tiles all at the plateau level, no level jump of two, all 41 gates straight, 0 of 3 724 crossings without stairs.
- **`town/townCore.ts`** (new) — `distanceToCore(town, x, y)`, the distance of a tile centre to the nearest core chunk, and `isOnPlateauWithoutWobble` (street tests).
- **`town/townTerrain.ts`** (new) — a town is a **core** (the footprint of whole chunks) plus a **margin** of `TOWN_MARGIN_TILES` (8) around it: the plateau is the Euclidean dilation of the core, so convex corners are rounded, concave corners stay sharp and the margins of two arms overlap. `townInfoAt(x, y)` reports a `zone` (`core`, `margin`, `ring`; `inside` is true for core and margin and for the pads, which count as `margin`) and only the core gets the town paving, the town biome, the layout and the lots (`terrainBlock.ts`, `townGrid.ts`); the margin keeps the ground of its biome, except where a street crosses it (`cityStreet`). Older text below that says "footprint" for the plateau means core plus margin now. `townInfoAt` (inside the core or the margin, or in the ring of `TOWN_RING_WIDTH_TILES` (8)
  around it, looked up in the tile's own cell only) and `levelWithTown(baseLevel, info)`: inside the level is the plateau
  level, in the ring a land level is limited to the plateau level minus up to plus `1 + floor(ringDistance / TOWN_RING_TERRACE_STEP_TILES)`
  (`TOWN_RING_TERRACE_STEP_TILES` 4 tiles per step, so the limit widens by one step every four tiles and neighbouring ring tiles never differ by more than one level; a step of 2 put a cliff every 2 tiles into the ring, too close for stairs),
  water stays water. `Chunk`
  uses it for the final level, and inside a footprint the ground is `city` paving, or `cityStreet` on a road tile (`groundOf`; the biome is still `TOWN_BIOME`, the meadow, for the decoration; a higher ground next to a town, like forest or jungle, draws its rim over it, water in the footprint
  becomes land). The outline is wavy: a tile of the margin is inside only when its depth below the margin edge (`TOWN_MARGIN_TILES` minus its distance to the core) is larger than a stepped noise value of 0 to `TOWN_OUTLINE_WOBBLE_TILES` (4, `outlineNoise`, frequency
  0.05, 1 octave: calm waves of about 20 tiles); eroded tiles count as ring, so the wobble only shrinks the plateau: the edge lies 4 to 8 tiles outside the core and never cuts into it (the ring is measured from the margin edge, not from the wobbled edge); a water tile in the eroded zone stays water. Measured on the straight
  outline of the old footprint, before the wobble and the margin (the margin was measured below, 2026-11-18): 858 015 neighbouring land tile pairs around 40 towns: no level jump of two, the footprints of
  neighbouring cells are at least 32 tiles apart, all footprint chunks lie in the inner 3 x 3 chunks.
- **`urban/urbanTable.ts`**, **`urban/urbanSheets.ts`** and **`town/townLayout.ts`** (new) — the first town content. `URBAN_ITEMS` is the catalog of buildings (plan in [idea.md](../idea.md), "Town layout and the urban catalog"): `name` (the file stem in `assets/urban/`), `kind` (`building`), `layer`, `sprite` (width and height in tiles, at most `URBAN_MAX_WIDTH_TILES` 9 x `URBAN_MAX_HEIGHT_TILES` 10, checked when the module loads; the sprite is the footprint until parts exist), `weight`, `unique` (placed once per town, first) and `minTownChunks`. Seven items so far: Pokemon Center (5 x 5, unique), Mart (4 x 4, unique, towns of two or more chunks) and five houses of 5 x 4. `scripts/cut_outside_urban.py` (run from `WorldNoise/`, needs Pillow) cuts them from the private `Outside` sheet (`assets/tilesets/outside.png`) into `assets/urban/<name>.png`; the art is not cleared for publishing and `assets/` stays git-ignored. `urbanImage(item)` is undefined until the image is loaded or when the PNG is missing, so a missing file draws nothing. `townBuildingsOf(town)` returns the placed buildings as `UrbanInstance`s (`item`, tile position, plateau level). The layout of a town is computed once (cached per town, `TOWN_CACHE_MAX_ENTRIES`) as a pure function
  of the town: tiles that are inside the wavy footprint and not road are free; the Center, the Mart (towns of two or more
  chunks) and `floor(chunks * TOWN_HOUSES_PER_CHUNK)` random houses are tried in an order of positions shuffled by the town's seeded random stream, and one
  fits where all its tiles are free and a door path exists. Tiles within `TOWN_BUILDING_STREET_MARGIN_TILES` (1) of a street are never free (a strip of paving along the street, the "sidewalk"). The path of a building is a strip along the whole south edge (`TOWN_DOOR_PATH_WIDTH_TILES` = 2 rows, as wide as the building; the door position of the sprite is not known, so the whole front is paved), which must be free or sidewalk when the building is placed and is reserved from then on, and a place only counts when a shortest path (breadth first, at most `TOWN_DOOR_PATH_MAX_TILES` = 14 tiles) leads from the strip to a sidewalk tile or to the strip of an earlier building. After all buildings are placed `town/townPathNetwork.ts` (`planPathNetwork`) joins them globally: the nodes are the sidewalk of the streets and every strip, the edges the shortest paths between them over the tiles that were free or sidewalk before the buildings, and the network is the minimum spanning tree (Prim from the street, ties by node order), so a building is connected once, to the street or to a nearer building. The connections are widened to 2 tiles; a building whose strip is not reached is dropped. The tiles are the side paths, drawn as `cityStreet` (`townPathAt(town, x, y)`, read by `Chunk`). A placed building blocks its tiles and a margin of `TOWN_BUILDING_MARGIN_TILES` (1); a building without a path is not placed there.
  A building that finds no place is left out, the Center too (a town may have no Center or Mart: decided with the user, not every town needs one). The road layer is read by the layout, never the other way round. `DecoRenderer` draws the buildings as sprites in the same sorted pass as the decoration (`collectBuildings` asks the towns of the cells in view; layer `object`, sorted by the bottom row of the footprint, tinted by the level shade); a town and the decoration never overlap, because the decoration keeps out of towns. The tile pieces of the sheet (`townDecorationAt`, `drawOutsideTile`) are gone. Not done: fountain, lamps, plaza fields (`cityGrass`), trees and fences; no fountain was found in the sheet yet.
- **Town plan: side streets, blocks and lots (urban plan, step 2).** `town/townGrid.ts` (`townGridOf(town)`, cached) holds what the layout and the plan share: the bounding box of the footprint in whole chunks, the road mask (`roads`) and `usable` (inside the wavy footprint, not road, not a crossing footprint). `town/townSideStreets.ts` (`planSideStreets`) walks along every straight segment of the through streets and, at hashed spacings (`TOWN_SIDE_STREET_MIN_SPACING_TILES` 12 .. `MAX` 20), tries a side street on each side with the chance `TOWN_SIDE_STREET_CHANCE` (0.8): `TOWN_SIDE_STREET_WIDTH_TILES` (2) wide, perpendicular, growing up to `TOWN_SIDE_STREET_MAX_LENGTH_TILES` (24) while its tiles are usable and the `TOWN_SIDE_STREET_EDGE_MARGIN_TILES` (2) ahead still lie in the town; shorter than `TOWN_SIDE_STREET_MIN_LENGTH_TILES` (6) it is dropped (the thin-spot rule). `town/townLots.ts` (`planLots`) takes the free tiles (usable and no side street), finds the **blocks** (4-connected components) and packs **lots** into each from the bottom row up and from the left: at an open tile the width is the first size of `splitSpan` over the open run (between `URBAN_LOT_MIN_WIDTH_TILES` 6 and `MAX` 11, a feasible split so no sliver is left), the depth the first size of `splitSpan` over the open rows above (`URBAN_LOT_MIN_DEPTH_TILES` 5 and `MAX` 12), so a lot always has the minimum size, lots never overlap and a ragged outline just leaves unlotted tiles (parks). A lot carries `frontage`: `through` or `side` when at least half of the row south of it is a through or side street, else `null` (buildings face south). `town/townPlan.ts` (`townPlanOf`, cached, one `Alea` stream per town) joins the three. Debug: `render/townPlanDebug.ts` draws side streets (blue), lots (yellow: fronts a through street, orange: a side street, pink: no street) with the debug mode (`r`). **Not painted yet:** the side streets are not in the road mask and the layout does not read the lots; both come with step 3 (the layout then places buildings into lots), so the picture still shows the old houses under the plan.
  Measured on 59 towns of 13 x 13 cells (one seed): 138 side streets (at least 6 long), 177 blocks, 806 lots (sizes 6 to 11 x 5 to 12), no lot tile on a street, outside the footprint or on another lot, the lots cover 77 % of the free tiles; 640 of the 806 lots have no street at their south edge (they depend on door paths or on step 4). Towns of 4 / 5 / 6 / 9 chunks have 10 / 10 / 16 / 27 lots.
- **`town/waypointNodes.ts`** (new) — `waypointPosition(cx, cy)`: the node of a cell without a town. 48 candidate tiles are drawn
  uniformly from the node area of the cell by hash (`node:cx:cy:index:x|y`); each gets a clearance, the Chebyshev distance to the nearest
  tile of another base level (`heightLevelAt` of the noise, not the cleaned levels and no crossings) or to water, searched up to 8 tiles
  (a tile in water has 0, a tile with nothing in reach 9); the candidate with the largest clearance wins, the lower index on a tie. The
  node area lies 16 tiles inside the cell and the radius is 8, so only the own cell is read. Cached per cell, about 1.7 ms per cell cold.
  Measured on 93 waypoint cells (13 x 13 cells, one seed): before (a random point) 21 % of all nodes lay in water and 28 % within 3 tiles of
  a level change; now none of the waypoint nodes lies in water (one would only if the whole node area were sea) and 3 of 93 have a clearance of 3 or less (16 candidates: 14 of 93, so 48).
  Stairs on the 309 edges of 1 024 x 1 024 tiles: 551 -> 571, failures of the kinds road curve 53 -> 15, no straight edge 79 -> 53, water 3 -> 1.
- **`town/townGates.ts`** (new) — `townGate(town, side)`: the gate of a town on a side (`gateSideOf(stepX, stepY)` turns the step to the
  neighbouring cell into east, west, south or north). Only the sides that have an edge are asked, so a town has as many gates as roads
  (measured on 76 towns of 13 x 13 cells: 35 with 2 gates, 28 with 3, 13 with 4; every node has at least two edges). The gate is the outermost plateau tile of its lane, `TOWN_MARGIN_TILES` (8) outside the core edge (the margin is straight there because the wobble tapers to 0), chosen before the outline wobble is built; only lanes with a straight core edge from `TOWN_GATE_STRAIGHT_EDGE_BEFORE_TILES` (5) before to `TOWN_GATE_STRAIGHT_EDGE_AFTER_TILES` (6) after them count (the rounded corners of the margin would cut the straight edge of the pad otherwise), unless a side has none (a 1 chunk arm of an L or T). Measured on 211 gates of 78 towns (14 x 14 cells): the plateau is straight 3 tiles before to 4 after the gate and 4 deep at every gate (56 were not straight before the filter). The lanes are the same as before otherwise: the lanes of the outermost footprint tiles of that side (the
  deepest row of the bounding box, so a notch of an L or T shape does not count), the middle third of them, and among those the lane with the
  lowest cost: water tiles in a strip of `TOWN_GATE_WATER_HALF_WIDTH_TILES` (4) each side and `TOWN_GATE_WATER_DEPTH_TILES` (14) outward times `TOWN_GATE_WATER_COST` (100; stairs need dry land) plus the number of tiles that differ in level from the one straight beyond the gate (`levelVariation`: `TOWN_GATE_LEVEL_HALF_WIDTH_TILES` = 2 each side, `TOWN_GATE_LEVEL_DEPTH_TILES` = 3 deep, level read as the base level limited to the plateau level +-1, the ring) so the stairs at the plateau edge find a straight edge (measured on 97 towns, 239 gates: gates with an unplanned crossing within 12 tiles 24, now 17); a tie is broken by hash (`gate:cx:cy:side`); cached per town and side. Opposite sides share one lane so a straight street can join them: the lane comes from the lanes both sides have (common lanes), in both middle thirds unless a lane outside them is cheaper, with the cost of both gates added and the hash key of the pair (`gate:cx:cy:east-west`, `gate:cx:cy:north-south`), so both sides compute the same lane; only where the two sides have no lane in common each side picks alone (measured on 1507 towns: all of them share a lane, before 7 %). It does not read the plateau outline, so
  `townTerrain` can read the gates: `gateTaperedWobble` takes the outline wobble down to 0 within `TOWN_GATE_STRAIGHT_HALF_WIDTH_TILES` (4)
  of the gate lane and lets it grow by one per tile beyond that, so the plateau edge is straight where the stairs go and the gate tile is always
  plateau (measured: all 304 gates of 76 towns). The taper is built for all four sides, whether the side has a road or not, so the plateau does
  not depend on the road graph.
- **`town/townNodes.ts`** (new) — `nodePosition(cx, cy)`: the centre of the town of the cell, else the waypoint. The road layer reads
  these; the town does not read the road. What is inside a town beyond lawn and the straight roads (houses, fountain, fences) is not
  built yet. The planned road (`road/roadPlanner.ts`) starts at the gate (the outermost tile of the margin) straight along its axis; the road of the graph ends there. **Shape shares** after the weights (701 towns, 40 x 40 cells): small 43 %, L 22 %, tall 10 %, wide 10 %, T 10 %, large 4 %, O 2 %. The shares differ from the weights because the big shapes (9 and 8 chunks) often miss the 97 % land rule and the town falls to the next kind in the order (before: small 35 %, L 21 %, T 14 %, large 11 %). **Land share lowered to 70 %** (decided with the user; water in the core becomes land like water in the margin, so a town may fill in a bay or a pond): shares of 1 013 towns on 40 x 40 cells small 32 %, L 19 %, wide 12 %, large 11 %, tall 11 %, T 10 %, O 5 % (at 85 %: 890 towns, large 8 %, O 4 %; at 97 % 701 towns), 90 % of the cells with the wish get a town (62 % at 97 %). Measured on 124 towns of 14 x 14 cells: no level jump of two, all 337 gates straight, 1 of 1 622 crossings without stairs (was 0), plateau levels 1 / 2 / 3 in 57 / 65 / 2 towns (coastal towns are low: the clearance of water around the core). The most watery core holds about 30 % water. **The gate pad was removed on 2026-11-18** (decided with the user: the margin is flat and straight at the gate, so it does the pad's job; `TOWN_GATE_PAD_DEPTH_TILES` is gone, the landing is 8 wide (`TOWN_GATE_LANDING_WIDTH_TILES`, `TOWN_GATE_LANDING_TILES_BEFORE_LANE` 3 before the lane) and starts right behind the gate, the stairs sit where the plateau meets it, `padsOf`, `padEndPoint` and the pad level source are gone, `townStreets` end at the gate). Measured after the change (78 towns, 14 x 14 cells): 211 gates straight, no level jump of two, 0 of 1638 crossings on 640 road edges without stairs. Where the text below says pad, read: the first four tiles behind the gate, now part of the landing and the ring. A **gate pad** (`town/townPads.ts`, `padsOf(town)`) is a rectangle outside the plateau edge in front of every gate that has an edge, the same for all four sides: `TOWN_GATE_PAD_DEPTH_TILES` (4) deep and `TOWN_GATE_PAD_WIDTH_TILES` (8) wide, `TOWN_GATE_PAD_TILES_BEFORE_LANE` (3) tiles before the gate lane and 4 after it, so it covers the 7 to 8 lanes of a stairs footprint (north/south 7, east/west 8 because of the bulge) and the 3 tiles of its length on the town side. It was 6 deep first (too long, decided with the user; measured: depth hardly changes the failures); at the plateau level. `townInfoAt` reports a pad tile as `inside` (plateau level, town ground, water becomes land) and measures the ring distance from the union of footprint and pads, so a cliff at a pad edge is at most one step high. `townLayout` ignores tiles outside its rectangle (`isInLayout`). The stairs find a straight pad edge; the gate lane cost reads the levels beyond the pad (`levelVariation`, depth 5 to 7, across the width of the pad). Behind every pad lies a **landing** (`landingsOf(town)`): the same width, `TOWN_GATE_LANDING_DEPTH_TILES` (6) deep, at one fixed level L (`TownTileInfo.fixedLevel`, water becomes land, the landing is not `inside`, so it keeps the biome ground). The stairs sit where the pad (level P) meets the landing, both sides flat, so the planner never has to flatten there and the cliff behind the gate stops being a problem (measured on 779 road edges, 24 x 24 cells: tiles without stairs 71 before, 12 now; at the gates of 97 towns 23 before, 0 now). L is decided by votes (`landingLevelOf`): every tile of the landing area votes with weight `TOWN_GATE_LANDING_AREA_VOTE_WEIGHT` (1) and the three rows behind it (`TOWN_GATE_LANDING_SAMPLE_DEPTH_TILES`) with 3, 2 and 1, with its base level limited to P-1 .. P+1; water votes for the lowest land level (P-1, at least 1), so a gate at the coast gets a beach landing and no long pier at P; a tie goes to the lowest level first, then P, then P+1. The terrace ring is a band now: every element (plateau, pad, landing) allows a difference of `1 + floor(distance / TOWN_RING_TERRACE_STEP_TILES)` around its own level, a ring tile takes the intersection of the bands of all elements within `TOWN_RING_WIDTH_TILES` (`ringBandAt`, `ringLow` and `ringHigh` of `TownTileInfo`), so the step stays one next to a landing at P +- 1 (without it 26 jumps of 2 steps appeared around 52 towns, now 0). The ring is cut at `TOWN_CELL_BUFFER_TILES` (16, the buffer of a cell) from the footprint, because a tile finds its town through its own cell; pad (4) plus landing (6) is checked against the buffer in `townPlacement.ts`. Not solved: 12 level changes without stairs on the 779 edges, none of them at a gate; 17 of 239 landings lie partly on water (a strip of land in the sea), 2 mostly. Inside the plateau the
  streets of `town/townStreets.ts` take over (`townStreets(town)`, a list of polylines, cached per town, added to `roadEdgesIn` as edges without crossings so the road mask paints them like roads): four gates give two crossing straight streets (west-east, north-south), three gates the straight street of the opposite pair plus a straight branch from the third gate at a right angle to it, two opposite gates one straight street, two neighbouring gates one street with a right-angled corner, one gate a dead end up to the centre line (never built so far: every node has at least two edges). Opposite gates share a lane, so the streets are straight; a jog in the middle exists in the code for the case that they do not. Measured on 1507 towns: no street leaves the footprint. Known limits the layout step has to deal with: `Chunk` hard-codes the
  town ground (`city` and `cityStreet` inside a footprint, no `cityGrass` yet) and `roadTilesIn` paints roads over the whole footprint, so a layout that
  owns its ground and roads needs a town-layer call that can replace or veto both; `townInfoAt` only says inside or ring
  (a fence or stairs step also needs the distance to the footprint edge and the base level); `Tile.elevation` stays the
  base value, so inside a footprint that was water it disagrees with the level and the ground (decoration must use the
  level and the town info); a ring tile keeps its base biome while its level is limited (the biome of a lowered or raised
  ring tile is not decided); the 97 % land check samples every second tile, so a one tile wide channel can be filled.
